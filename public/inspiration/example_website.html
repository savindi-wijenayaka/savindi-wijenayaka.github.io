Hi, I'm Savindi — a Machine Learning Engineer passionate about turning research into reliable, real-world systems. I work across the full ML lifecycle, from experimenting with novel architectures to deploying and monitoring models in production.

My work spans NLP and large language models, computer vision, and MLOps. I care deeply about model interpretability, reproducibility, and building systems that are not just accurate but trustworthy.
